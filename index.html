Scout helps you study all 128 questions of the 2025 U.S. civics test, in English and Spanish, and practice answering them out loud.

Need help? Email jp@thecloudnomad.com.

“Tap and speak” says the microphone is off

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, find Scout, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can always show the answers and grade yourself instead.

A video won’t play

Videos need an internet connection, so check yours and tap Try again. Lessons work without the video.

Which test does the app cover?

The 2025 civics test, which applies if you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025. If you filed before that date, you’ll take the 2008 version with 100 questions, which this app doesn’t cover.

Are the answers official?

The questions and answers come from USCIS’s public study materials, form M-1778. Names of current officials can change, so check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.

How do I erase my progress?

In the app, open Settings and tap Reset progress. Your progress is only on your device, so deleting the app also erases it.
