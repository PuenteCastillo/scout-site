Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Scout: US Citizenship Test is made by Cloud Nomads LLC (“we”). It has no accounts, ads or tracking. It doesn’t ask for your name or email, and your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress and scores, your settings, the state you choose, your interview date, whether you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, whether the 65/20 rule applies to you, and anything you type as an answer, such as the name of your representative.

This is saved only on your device, and we can’t see it. Reset progress in Settings erases it, and so does deleting the app. If you back up your device, the backup may include it.

When you answer by voice

When you tap “Tap and speak,” your voice goes to Apple’s speech recognition, which turns it into text. Apple may process it on your device or on its servers, as the permission prompt explains. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

The app uses the text to check your answer and doesn’t keep it. We never receive your voice or the text. You can turn off the microphone or speech recognition in your device’s Settings at any time, and you can always show the answers and grade yourself instead.

When you watch a video

Lesson videos are streamed from Bunny Stream, our video host. Like any website, its servers receive your IP address and basic device information so they can deliver the video, and they may log it. We can see overall viewing totals, but not who watched.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. They don’t identify you.

What we don’t do

We don’t collect your name, email address, contacts, location or immigration status. We don’t sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps. Nothing you enter is sent to USCIS or any other government agency.

Links to sites such as uscis.gov, senate.gov and usa.gov open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

This app is made for adults preparing for the naturalization interview. It isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
